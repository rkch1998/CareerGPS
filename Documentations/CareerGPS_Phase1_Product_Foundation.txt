
CAREERGPS - PHASE 1
PRODUCT FOUNDATION

1. VISION
CareerGPS helps students discover, compare, and navigate education and career paths.

2. MISSION
Help every student make informed career decisions.

3. TARGET USERS
- Class 10 Students
- Class 12 Students
- Parents
- Teachers
- Career Counsellors

4. USER PERSONAS
A. Class 10 Student
- Confused about stream selection
- Doesn't know available careers

B. Parent
- Wants stable future
- Needs trusted information

C. Class 12 Student
- College and exam decisions

5. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
- Student Comes First
- Recommend, Never Decide
- Explain Every Recommendation
- Show Multiple Paths
- Respect Reality
- Avoid Prestige Bias
- Avoid Salary-Only Bias
- Encourage Exploration

6. LANGUAGE GUIDE
- Class 8 student should understand every question
- Use simple language
- No jargon
- One idea per question
- Friendly tone

7. THREE MODES

Explore Mode
- Discover careers
- 2-3 minutes

Journey Mode
- Main recommendation flow
- 8-12 minutes

Discovery Mode
- Deep self-discovery
- 20-30 minutes

8. DECISION FRAMEWORK

Level 1: Current Situation
Level 2: Learning Preferences
Level 3: Interests
Level 4: Lifestyle Goals
Level 5: Constraints
Level 6: Career Preferences
Level 7: Recommendations

9. ETHICAL PRINCIPLES
- Student decides
- System recommends
- Explain reasoning
- Show alternatives
- Respect every profession

10. FOUNDER PARKING LOT
- Career Decision Engine
- Family Decision Support Tool
- AI Career Mentor
- Parent Reports
