
CAREERGPS - FOUNDATION DOCUMENTS

==================================================
1. VISION
==================================================
CareerGPS helps students discover, compare, and navigate education and career paths based on their interests, goals, strengths, and real-world circumstances.

Core idea:
"Tell us where you are today and where you want to go. We'll help you find the possible routes."

==================================================
2. MISSION
==================================================
To help every student make informed career decisions through personalized, visual, understandable, and trustworthy guidance.

==================================================
3. PRD SUMMARY
==================================================
CareerGPS is a Career Decision Platform.

Primary Users:
- Class 10 students
- Class 12 students

Secondary Users:
- Parents
- Teachers
- Career Counsellors

Core Features:
- Career Roadmaps
- Career Explorer
- Route Comparison
- Personalized Recommendations
- Backup Plans
- Career Details

==================================================

1. Student Comes First

3. Explain Every Recommendation

6. Avoid Prestige Bias
7. Avoid Salary-Only Bias
8. Be Honest About Uncertainty
9. Encourage Exploration
10. Every Career Deserves Respect

==================================================
6. LANGUAGE GUIDE
==================================================

Rule:
A Class 8 student should understand every question.

Examples:

Bad:
"What is your aptitude?"

Good:
"What kind of work sounds fun to you?"

Rules:
- Use simple words
- One idea per question
- No jargon
- Friendly tone
- Explain why questions are asked

==================================================
7. DECISION FRAMEWORK
==================================================

Level 1:
Current Situation

Level 2:
Learning Preferences

Level 3:
Interests

Level 4:
Lifestyle Goals

Level 5:
Constraints

Level 6:
Career Preferences

Level 7:
Recommendations

Framework Flow:

Question
→ Answer
→ Meaning
→ Evidence
→ Career Traits
→ Routes
→ Explanation

==================================================
8. THREE MODES
==================================================

Explore Mode
- 2-3 minutes
- Discover careers

Journey Mode
- 8-12 minutes
- Main CareerGPS experience

Discovery Mode
- 20-30 minutes
- Deep self-discovery

==================================================
9. ETHICAL PRINCIPLES
==================================================

- No black-box recommendations
- No ranking careers by prestige
- No ranking careers only by salary
- Show uncertainty honestly
- Show alternatives
- Student makes final decision

==================================================

CareerGPS should help students:

Discover
→ Understand
→ Navigate
→ Grow

CareerGPS should not simply answer:

"What should I become?"
