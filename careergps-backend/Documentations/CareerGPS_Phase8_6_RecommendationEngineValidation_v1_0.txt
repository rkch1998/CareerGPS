==================================================
VALIDATION RULES
==================================================

Rule 1

Different students should receive different recommendations.

--------------------------------------------------

Rule 2

No career should dominate every recommendation list.

--------------------------------------------------

Rule 3

Recommendations must be explainable.

--------------------------------------------------

Rule 4

Students must always receive alternative options.

--------------------------------------------------

Rule 5

Never display:

"Perfect Career"

"Best Career"

"Only Career"

--------------------------------------------------

Rule 6

If confidence is low:

Recommend exploration instead of prediction.

==================================================
SUCCESS CRITERIA
==================================================

✓ Recommendations make logical sense

✓ Results are explainable

✓ Students can discover new careers

✓ Recommendations avoid bias

✓ Students remain in control
