
CAREERGPS - PHASE 3
RULE-BASED RECOMMENDATION ENGINE

GOAL
Convert student answers into meaningful career recommendations using transparent rules.

WHY RULE-BASED?
- Easy to explain
- Easy to validate
- No training data required
- Suitable for educational decisions
