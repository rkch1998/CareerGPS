CAREERGPS - PHASE 6
QUESTION BANK v1.0

PURPOSE

Collect student information in a simple, friendly, and understandable way.

RULES

1. Questions must be understandable by a Class 8 student.
2. Avoid technical jargon.
3. One question should ask only one thing.
4. Questions should feel conversational.
5. Every answer should map to Interests, Skills, Traits, or Career Categories.
